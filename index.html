    Who needs GST registration?

    Generally, businesses whose annual turnover crosses ₹40 lakh for goods or ₹20 lakh for services, with lower limits in some states. Inter-state suppliers of goods and most online sellers must register regardless of turnover. We confirm what applies to your business.

    Should I choose the old or the new tax regime?

    It depends on your deductions. Try the calculator above for an estimate. Salaried individuals can choose afresh every year; those with business income have limited chances to switch, so that choice deserves a careful look.

    When is the income tax return due?

    Usually 31 July for individuals who don't need an audit, and 31 October for audit cases. A belated or revised return can generally be filed up to 31 December, with a late fee for belated returns. Dates are sometimes extended by notification.

    How long does company or LLP registration take?

    Once documents and digital signatures are ready, incorporation usually takes two to three weeks, depending on name approval and processing by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

    I've received a notice from the tax department. Can you help?

    Yes. We review the notice, explain what it means, prepare the reply and represent you where needed, for both income tax and GST. Share the notice with us as early as possible; most have strict response deadlines.

    How are your fees decided?

    Fees depend on the scope and complexity of the work. We understand your requirement first and confirm the fee in writing before we begin.

    Can I work with you if I live in another city?

    Yes. Most work is handled over phone and email, with documents shared securely. You're welcome to meet us at the office whenever you prefer.

    Is my information kept confidential?

    Yes. Chartered Accountants are bound by professional confidentiality. Your documents are used only for your engagement and shared only through the channel we agree with you.
