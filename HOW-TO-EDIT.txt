HOW TO EDIT YOUR WEBSITE
========================

The website is two files:
  index.html       the whole page (design, text, calculator)
  site-config.js   your details (name, phone, address, email key)

Open either file in a plain text editor:
  Windows: Notepad, or VS Code (free)
  Mac: TextEdit in plain-text mode, or VS Code (free)
Never open them in Word; it will break the code.


1. YOUR DETAILS  ->  edit site-config.js
------------------------------------------
Type your details between the quotes and save. Example:

  firmName: "Rao & Associates",
  phone: "+91 98765 43210",
  whatsapp: "919876543210",

Keep the quotes and the comma at the end of each line.
This fills in the header, top bar, contact card, footer, WhatsApp
buttons and call buttons everywhere on the page.


2. MAKE THE REQUEST FORM EMAIL YOU
-----------------------------------
a) Go to https://web3forms.com
b) Enter the email address that should receive service requests.
c) They email you an "Access Key". Copy it.
d) Paste it into site-config.js:   web3formsKey: "your-key-here",
e) Save, re-upload, and send yourself a test request.

Until the key is added, the form tells visitors to call or WhatsApp.


3. CHANGING TEXT ON THE PAGE  ->  edit index.html
--------------------------------------------------
Use Find (Ctrl+F / Cmd+F) to locate the exact words you want to
change, then type over them. Only change the words between > and <.

  Example: find  Tax filings and accounts, kept in order.
           replace it with your own headline.

Common things to find:
  Hero headline ........ "kept in order"
  Service cards ........ the service name, e.g. "Income tax returns"
  Due dates table ...... "GSTR-3B for September"  (update every month)
  FAQ answers .......... the question text, e.g. "How are your fees decided"
  Tax slabs ............ in index.html search "NEW_SLABS" (update after
                         every Budget; only if the government changes them)


4. RULES TO STAY ICAI-COMPLIANT
-------------------------------
Do NOT add: fees or prices (even "free"), testimonials, client names,
"why choose us", claims like "best", "100% accuracy", "10,000+ filings",
or years of experience as a selling point. Check the current ICAI
website guidelines before adding anything promotional.


5. PUBLISHING CHANGES
---------------------
After editing, upload the folder again the same way you first did
(see the steps Claude gave you). The site updates in about a minute.
